# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Stratos project a welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Focus on technical clarity and assume good intent
- Welcome newcomers and help them get started
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or bad-faith arguments
- Publishing others' private information without consent

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to **conduct@stratos.dev**. All reports will be reviewed and will result in a response appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct may face repercussions as determined by the project leadership.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other communication channel associated with Stratos.
