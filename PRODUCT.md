# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People working with coding agents on macOS, moving between repositories, agents, and conversations during sustained work sessions.

## Product Purpose

Stratos brings agent conversations and their tools into one desktop workspace. Users should always be able to find a thread, start another, and return to their draft.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, approachable. Codex and Conductor are references for a compact, polished desktop experience.

## Anti-references

Avoid excessive cards, loud borders, decorative gradients, and navigation controls hidden behind native window buttons.

## Design Principles

- Keep navigation discoverable in every view.
- Let the conversation be the focus.
- Make the refined appearance optional without disabling usability fixes.
- Preserve drafts and ongoing work when switching views or appearance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard operation, visible focus, readable contrast, and both light and dark appearance. Do not rely on color alone for state.
